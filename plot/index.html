Where the machine stands

Machine
NextDraw 1117, firmware EBB 3.0.3, on the render PC
Software
Inkscape 1.4.4 with the NextDraw 1.5.0 extension, nextdraw CLI, vpype 1.15
Model flag
-L9 on every call. The default targets a different machine.
Pen
Paper Mate Profile ballpoint. Down positions 40, 20 and 10 all drew too light; next runs start at full pressure and slower.
Clearance
Pen tip 2 to 4 mm clear when up, or it drags travel lines.
Camera
Logitech C920 over the bed for captures

Calibration sheet codes

Every test pattern is printed with a code. Give feedback by code, for example "B3 too faint, C5 clean".
